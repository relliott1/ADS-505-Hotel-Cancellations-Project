The problem statement for this project is:
Hotels face significant revenue loss and inventory management challenges due to high reservation cancellation rates.  Currently,
the key drivers behind booking cancellations are not fully defined, and therefore the hotel lacks a reliable method to proactively
identify high-risk reservations or link cancellation probabilities directly to revenue outcomes.

Project Description
The Hotel Booking Demand dataset contains 119,390 hotel bookings and 32 variables from a resort hotel and a city hotel. The data include booking characteristics such as lead time, length of stay, number of guests, market segment, deposit type, room type, previous cancellations, special requests, and whether the booking was canceled. The bookings cover arrival dates from July 2015 through August 2017.
